Jalali Persian Calendar is a local calendar app for Android that displays Gregorian and Persian (Jalali) dates. It works without a network connection or account and does not track you.

View each Gregorian month with Gregorian or Jalali day numbers, with weekends marked. Swipe between months, or tap Today to return to the current date.

Tap a day to add an event, or to see the events it already has. An event can repeat every year on the same Gregorian date. Events are saved on your phone; the app has no internet access and never sends them anywhere.

A built-in converter turns a Gregorian date into its Jalali equivalent and back, updating as you type.

The app also shows the current time in Iran, updated in real time, alongside the calendar.

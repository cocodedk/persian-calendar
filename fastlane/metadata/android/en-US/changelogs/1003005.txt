The app is now called Jalali Persian Calendar, so it can't be confused with another app of the same name.

Date converter fixes:
- The month field waits for a second digit, so you can type 10, 11 and 12.
- The keyboard's Next key moves to the next field instead of skipping one.
- Dates that don't exist, such as 30 Esfand 1402, now show "Invalid Date" instead of a wrong answer.
- The result and the "Invalid Date" message stay above the keyboard.
